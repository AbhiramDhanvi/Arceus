# ARCEUS design

ARCEUS uses the pacing of a public-service field guide. It opens on a person asking for help, explains the eligibility interview through one worked example, then gives visitors direct access to the scheme directory and family workspace.

- **Hero.** A deep green photographic backdrop, stable text, cursor-responsive light, and a separately animated voice control. The waveform settles after the introduction. Mobile uses a lighter asset and a tighter crop.
- **Scroll.** Native scrolling advances the healthcare example from speech to rule checks to the government portal. A pinned panel changes as each step enters view, and a line tracks progress. The three explanations stay in the document flow. Reduced motion uses a static panel and full text.
- **System.** Warm paper, deep green, and citron tie navigation, focus, audio, and application surfaces together. Instrument Serif is used for English display text. Noto Sans Devanagari and Telugu keep their scripts readable. Functional screens use stable layouts and shorter transitions.
- **Attached skill references.** Scroll Craft informed depth and the pinned journey. Awwwards and Jury informed the focal interaction and desktop, mobile, keyboard, and reduced-motion review. Taste informed typography, restrained colour, content rhythm, and touch targets. The AI Research Skills package informed the worked example through its problem reformulation method.
